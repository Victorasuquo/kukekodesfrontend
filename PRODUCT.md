# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KukeKodes serves learners, instructors, organization administrators, and platform administrators. Many learners use low-bandwidth or intermittently connected networks, may share a household contact email, and need a clear path back into a lesson without losing progress.

## Product Purpose

KukeKodes provides structured coding courses, assignments, assessments, progress tracking, certificates, moderated learning communities, live-session links, and course-aware coaching. Success means each role can complete its core work using real persisted data, with truthful feedback when a service or connection is unavailable.

## Brand Personality

Practical, encouraging, and trustworthy. The product should feel approachable to a first-time learner while remaining precise enough for instructors and administrators.

## Anti-references

Avoid placeholder-heavy demos, invented scale or compliance claims, dead navigation, fake success states, decorative dashboard statistics, and unfamiliar controls that obscure standard learning or administration tasks.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real system state and always provide a recovery action.
2. Keep the learning path readable and usable on constrained connections.
3. Separate learner, organization, and platform authority visibly and technically.
4. Prefer familiar, accessible controls that help users finish a task.
5. Preserve learner work across refreshes, retries, and reconnects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, zoom and text expansion, high-contrast states, low-data lesson access, and explicit offline or slow-network messaging.
